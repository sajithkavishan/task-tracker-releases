# Task Tracker

A simple, dark-themed Windows app for planning your tasks and tracking the time you spend on them.

- Tasks and subtasks, with notes
- A timer for every task, and a small always-on-top timer that stays above your other apps
- Break reminders and task reminders
- Calendar and daily / weekly stats
- Dark and light themes
- Daily automatic backups and automatic updates

## Download

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/sajithkavishan/task-tracker-releases/releases/latest)**

On that page, under **Assets**, click the file ending in **`_x64-setup.exe`** (for example `TaskTracker_1.0.0_x64-setup.exe`).

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).

## Install

1. Open the downloaded `setup.exe` file.
2. Windows may show a blue **"Windows protected your PC"** message. This appears because the app isn't signed with a paid code-signing certificate — it doesn't mean anything is wrong. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Follow the installer. Task Tracker opens when it's done.

## Updates

You only need to install once. When a new version is released, the app shows **"ready to install"** — click **Install** and it updates itself. You can also check any time in **Settings → About → Check for updates**.

## Your data

- Your tasks are stored on your PC and are kept when you update or reinstall the app.
- A backup is saved every day to `Documents\Task Tracker Backups` (the last 14 days are kept).
- **Settings → Your data** lets you export everything to a file and import it again — for example to move to a new PC.

## Uninstall

Open **Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps**, find **Task Tracker** and choose **Uninstall**. Your data is kept unless you tick **"Delete application data"** in the uninstaller.
